To the extent they hold the relevant rights, SRG contributors dedicate original
project documentation to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal (`CC0-1.0`).
This covers original prose in `docs/`, root Markdown documents such as
`CONTRIBUTING.md` and this notice, and original asset-register metadata.
Executable code examples use Apache-2.0 as specified above.

See the complete [CC0 legal text](LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt) and its
[official source](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode).

where the waiver is ineffective. Attribution to SRG is welcome but is not a
condition of this dedication. This notice does not waive other people's rights
or grant trademark or patent rights under CC0.

## External and separately reviewed material

These project-wide grants do not relicense third-party papers, datasets, author
code, dependencies, copied figures/tables, quotations, or their protected
content embedded in documentation or metadata. License texts retain their
own terms. Preserve specific notices and record third-party assets in the
[asset-rights register](docs/asset-rights/README.md).

Study data, derived datasets, and packaged research artifacts require an
explicit rights review and distribution decision; they do not acquire CC0
merely by being stored under `docs/` or referenced in the register. Separate
SRG-authored metadata from copied source content and identify exceptions at
the asset or excerpt. Unknown or conflicting terms must be resolved before
redistribution. Public access alone never implies redistribution permission.

Code and documentation contributions follow these respective terms as described
in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Changes to this policy require a recorded
maintainer decision and review of the rights needed for affected contributions;
editing this notice does not automatically relicense earlier third-party work.
