# ADR 0001: SRG v0.1 proof-of-concept scope

- Status: Proposed; awaiting maintainer ratification
- Date: 2026-09-05
- Tracks: [Issue #1 — T01](https://github.com/gle-mini/statistical-replication-gym/issues/1)
- Roadmap: SRG plan v2, milestones M0–M5

## Context

Statistical Replication Gym (SRG) aims to evaluate whether an agent can
reconstruct statistical analyses from source evidence and produce auditable
conclusions. Agreement with published numbers alone cannot establish that the
implementation is sound or that a scientific claim is valid.

The first release needs a bounded scope that can be independently reviewed
before broader robustness analysis, training experiments, or formal assurance.
This decision defines intended v0.1 behavior and release requirements; it does
not assert that any capability has already been implemented or validated.

## Decision

### Supported analyses

SRG v0.1 targets ordinary least squares (OLS) and binary logistic regression.
Two synthetic calibration tasks, one for each model family, precede evaluation
on real studies. Their reference results must come from analytic or independent
calculations, rather than snapshots of the implementation being tested.

Each admitted study must document its sample construction, preprocessing,
model specification, uncertainty conventions, and target tolerances. Support
for a model family does not imply support for every estimator, diagnostic, or
software convention associated with it. Missing or unsupported behavior must
be reported explicitly. Separation, non-convergence, worker failures, and
missing outputs must not be presented as successful reproduction.

Studies requiring methods beyond this scope need an explicit scope decision
before admission; their requirements must not silently expand v0.1.

### Four distinct evidence layers

| Layer | Question and required evidence | Limit of the claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Faithful reproduction | Does the reconstructed analysis reproduce the specified sample, methods, and reported targets? Compare fresh execution outputs with located paper targets and separately identified adjudicated reference values, using justified tolerances. | Numerical agreement does not establish implementation integrity or scientific validity. |
| Implementation integrity | Does the submitted code actually compute the claimed results within the allowed environment? Use external reruns, intermediate artifacts, provenance, negative controls, and applicable functional probes. | Passing integrity checks does not validate the study's scientific assumptions. |
| Methodological assessment | What do source evidence and supported data/model diagnostics justify about assumptions and claims? Link findings to artifacts and state ambiguities, unsupported checks, and reasons for abstention. | Passing diagnostics is not a universal validity claim; a concern is not an allegation of misconduct. |
| Robustness | How do conclusions change under justified alternative specifications? Assess alternatives separately from faithful reconstruction and identify changes to the estimand. | Reproducing one specification does not demonstrate robustness; specification frequency is not a probability that a claim is true. |

v0.1 implements and reports the first three layers within its declared support.
Robustness remains a distinct layer in the conceptual model, but executing a
bounded multiverse is deferred to v0.2. v0.1 reports must make that limitation
explicit rather than imply that robustness has been established.

Paper-reported values and adjudicated reference results remain separate.
Reports distinguish reproduced, discrepant, under-specified, blocked, and
not-assessable outcomes. Infrastructure and submission failures are separate
from scientific discrepancies. A justified non-match or abstention can be the
correct result.

### Public v0.1 target and release boundary

The public v0.1 target is **three independently reviewed real studies**, in
addition to the two synthetic calibration tasks. Candidate triage records
three primary candidates and two backups. At least one primary study must
have usable author code and at least one must require reconstruction without
usable author code.

Each released study must have 5–15 precisely located targets, checksummed
inputs, explicit asset rights, a documented reconstruction, justified frozen
tolerances, and an adjudicated reference independently rerun and reviewed by a
second reviewer. Reference checks must pass three clean reruns. If a natural
discrepant or blocked case is unavailable, a clearly labeled synthetic variant
may calibrate that behavior; it does not replace one of the three real studies.

M0–M5 remain the v0.1 release path: scope and governance, contracts and
synthetic tasks, isolated agent execution and external evaluation, reviewed
real studies, diagnostics and baselines, then packaging and independent
reproduction of the release. The framework-neutral agent episode protocol,
integrity checks, provenance, and reference/negative/LLM baselines are part of
this path. The detailed milestone gates remain applicable; three task packages
alone do not constitute a completed release.

The corpus is public development/evaluation material. It is not a
contamination-free held-out benchmark and does not establish training/evaluation
independence or broad performance across scientific studies. Only artifacts
with appropriate redistribution rights may be published; public accessibility
alone does not establish those rights.

### Deferred work

- Bounded multiverse and robustness execution belongs to M6 / v0.2.
- RL training and optional ecosystem adapters belong to conditional M7 work.
  Training requires additional reviewed, disjoint study groups and a frozen
  held-out evaluation protocol; the three public v0.1 studies are insufficient.
- Formal proof belongs to the independent M8 research extension. Any later
  proof must identify its exact property and proof boundary.

None of these extensions is required to release v0.1. The v0.1 interactive
agent environment and LLM evaluation baselines do not constitute RL training.

### Reward policy

Aggregate reward weights remain **provisional until calibration** under
[T11 / issue #11](https://github.com/gle-mini/statistical-replication-gym/issues/11).
This decision fixes neither numeric weights nor a scoring formula.

Evaluation must expose component rewards alongside any aggregate and document
normalization and treatment of missing, failed, unsupported, blocked,
discrepant, and not-assessable outcomes. Calibration must check independently
known references and negative controls, including cases where justified
abstention or discrepancy deserves more credit than fabricated agreement.
Freeze and record evaluator/reward versions for the baseline report; subsequent
reward changes require a new version.

## Ratification and change process

The maintainer accepts or amends this decision before downstream work relies
on it as frozen scope. Record acceptance in this document with its date and a
link to the review or decision evidence. Until then, its status is Proposed.

For a substantive scope change:

1. Open an issue or pull request explaining the reason, proposed change, and
   impact on studies, acceptance criteria, release gates, and existing results.
2. Obtain maintainer review and record the decision. Changes to scientific
   references or findings also follow the independent review procedure once
   defined under T04.
3. Add a superseding decision record for a change to this scope; retain this
   record and link the replacement. Editorial clarifications may amend this
   file with the rationale recorded in version control.
4. Update affected issues and documentation together. Version affected tasks,
   evaluators, and rewards as applicable, and identify results requiring reruns.
   Preserve the versions underlying previously published reports.

## Consequences and follow-up decisions

This scope favors a small, auditable proof of concept. It limits the model
families and conclusions that v0.1 can advertise and makes independent review
and reproducible evidence release requirements.

The following decisions remain open and are not resolved by this record:

- T02 / [#2](https://github.com/gle-mini/statistical-replication-gym/issues/2):
  code and documentation licenses, contribution rules, and asset rights.
- T03 / [#3](https://github.com/gle-mini/statistical-replication-gym/issues/3):
  named study candidates and admission feasibility.
- T04 / [#4](https://github.com/gle-mini/statistical-replication-gym/issues/4):
  detailed review, dispute, and correction procedures.

  calibrated reward components, weights, and normalization.

Ratifying this scope does not complete the other M0 tasks or its milestone gate.
